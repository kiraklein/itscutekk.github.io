Prototype

AI risk assessment

Answer ten questions. Velceron will generate a first-pass governance view. Nothing is sent to a server.

1. Does the system make or materially influence decisions about people?
2. Does it handle personal, financial, health or other sensitive data?
3. Can the AI take external actions such as sending, changing or deleting data?
4. Is there meaningful human review before consequential output is used?
5. Are model limitations, intended use and prohibited use documented?
6. Have quality, bias, failure-mode or adversarial tests been recorded?
7. Are inputs, outputs, model/version and key decisions traceable?
8. Is there an accountable business owner and technical owner?
9. Is ongoing monitoring defined for drift, incidents and harmful outcomes?
10. Can the system be paused, overridden or rolled back quickly?
